About this app

This app puts every CAP assignment, every rubric criterion, and your progress in your pocket. Browse the menu, review what each assignment requires, capture photos or videos in the moment, and hand off to Canvas to submit.

Submissions never touch our servers. Files go straight from your phone to Canvas through your phone's share sheet — same way you'd send a photo to a friend.

Your name

This appears on shared submissions to make grading easier for your professor.

How to submit

Each assignment has a Submit button. Tap it, choose what you're attaching (photo, video, written response, or other file), pick from your phone's gallery, then either tap Open Canvas (recommended) or Share Files to finish the submission inside Canvas.
